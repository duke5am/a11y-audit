About us

Northwind Widgets was founded in 1998 by two toolmakers who were tired of waiting three weeks for a bearing. We started with a single shelf in a shared unit on the Waalhaven and now run a 4,000 m² warehouse with 26 staff.

How we work

Every catalogue part has a named engineer behind it. If a drawing does not match the part you receive, that engineer picks up the phone.
